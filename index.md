# HookTunnel Documentation

Welcome to the official HookTunnel documentation. Learn how to capture, inspect, debug, and replay webhooks during development.

---

## Get Started in 30 Seconds

```
1. Go to hooktunnel.com
2. Click "Create Hook"
3. Copy your unique webhook URL
4. Paste it into your provider's webhook settings
5. Watch events appear in real-time
```

That's it. No account required for basic usage.

## Popular Guides

### [Smart Search](/features/semantic-search/)
Find webhooks by meaning, not just text. Search for "stripe payment failed" and find relevant requests instantly.

### Webhook Capture
Create unique URLs for Stripe, Twilio, GitHub, and any webhook provider.

### Request Replay (Pro)
Re-send captured webhooks to your local server with one click.
